# Seam Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 4 November 2026_

Seam is a wardrobe app that runs entirely on your phone. This page explains, in plain words, what the app does with your information.

## What Seam stores

- **Your closet**: the clothes you add (name, category, colour, price, how often you wear them) and the photos you take or choose.
- **Your settings**: whether weather is on, and your temperature unit.

All of this is stored **only on your device**. Seam has no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers of its own. We cannot see your closet, your photos, or anything else in the app.

## Photos and camera

Seam asks for camera or photo-library access only when you add a piece. Photos are resized and saved inside the app on your phone. They are never uploaded anywhere.

## Location and weather

If you allow it, Seam uses your **approximate location** once per hour while the app is open to fetch today's weather from [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com), a free weather service. Only your rounded latitude and longitude (to two decimal places, roughly 1 km) are sent, with no name, ID, or other personal data. Your location is never stored by Seam. You can turn weather off in Settings at any time, and the app works without it.

## What Seam does not do

- No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
- No analytics or tracking.
- No selling or sharing of data with anyone.
- No third-party SDKs other than the open-source libraries needed to run the app.

## Deleting your data

Open **Settings → Delete everything** inside the app, or simply delete the app from your phone. Either removes all closet data and photos.

## Children

Seam is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
