# Seam Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 6 November 2026_

Seam is a wardrobe app that runs entirely on your phone. This page explains, in plain words, what the app does with your information.

## What Seam stores

- **Your closet**: the clothes you add (name, kind, colour, warmth, price, status such as "in laundry", how often and when you wore them) and their photos.
- **What you wore**: each outfit you log, by day, and any photo of the look you add.
- **Trips**: trip names, types, destinations, dates and your packing lists.
- **Your style**: a short summary of your style that Seam works out from your closet and from photos of you that you choose to add.
- **Your settings**: weather on or off, temperature unit, the morning reminder, and whether you have Seam Plus.

All of this is stored **only on your device**. Seam has no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers of its own. We cannot see your closet, your photos, or anything else in the app.

## Photos and camera

Seam asks for camera or photo access only when you add a piece or a photo of a look. Photos, including photos of you, are processed **on your phone** (by Apple's Vision framework and, where available, Apple Intelligence) to cut out a garment, read its colour or recognise what you are wearing. They are saved inside the app and never uploaded anywhere.

## The stylist

The stylist, photo recognition and label scanning use Apple Intelligence **on your iPhone**. Your questions and photos are not sent to Seam or to any server we run.

## Location and weather

If you allow it, Seam uses your **approximate location** about once an hour while the app is open to fetch the weather from [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com), a free weather service. Only your rounded latitude and longitude (two decimal places, roughly 1 km) are sent, with no name, ID or other personal data.

When you plan a trip, the place names you type and the destinations you pick are sent to Open-Meteo's place search and forecast services to find the place and its weather.

Open-Meteo receives these requests like any website receives a visit, including your IP address. Seam never stores your location. You can turn weather off in Settings at any time, and the app works without it.

## Notifications

If you switch on the morning reminder, Seam schedules a local notification on your phone. No push service is used.

## Purchases

Seam Plus is sold and billed by Apple through the App Store. Seam never sees your payment details. The app only learns from Apple whether your subscription is active.

## What Seam does not do

- No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
- No analytics or tracking.
- No selling or sharing of data with anyone.
- No third-party SDKs other than the open-source libraries needed to run the app.

## Deleting your data

Delete the app from your phone. That removes your closet, photos, outfits and trips; nothing is kept anywhere else. You can also remove any single piece from its page.

## Children

Seam is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
